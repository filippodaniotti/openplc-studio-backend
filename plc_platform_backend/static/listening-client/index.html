MUSHRA listening test

Use headphones in a quiet environment. Compare each anonymous stimulus with the reference. Rate quality from 0 (bad) to 100 (excellent). Untouched ratings are unanswered, not zero.

Break recommended after each ten trials. Your acknowledged answers are saved; pause now and resume later using your assigned invitation link.

Quality scale: 0–20 bad · 20–40 poor · 40–60 fair · 60–80 good · 80–100 excellent.

Keyboard controls — highlighted track
  • Space — Play / pause.
  • ← — Move playback to the beginning.
  • ↑ / ↓ — Increase / decrease the score by 5.
  • Shift + ↑ / Shift + ↓ — Increase / decrease the score by 20.
  • Tab — Focus the next track.
  • Shift + Tab — Focus the previous track.
  • Enter — Next trial, once all ratings are confirmed.
  • R — Toggle repeat playback.
  • Esc — Leave track navigation to Tab through other controls and links.
Focusing and rating tracks

Click anywhere on a track to focus it. The focused track is highlighted. The reference cannot be scored. Play each stimulus before scoring; changing its score confirms it. To keep the starting score of 50, click “Confirm current value”.

Waiting for your listening session…

Sliders start at 50 but remain unanswered until you interact or explicitly confirm. Play a stimulus before rating it. Save acknowledgements are required before advancing or finishing.
